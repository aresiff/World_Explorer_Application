Overview of the application and its purpose:

The application, World Explorer, is a comprehensive travel planning tool designed for travellers who want to learn more about their destinations and surrounding countries. 
By integrating the REST Countries API, the app provides detailed country-specific information, such as:
- Geographical Data: Area, continent, region, and subregion to give travellers a sense of where each country is located globally.
- Administrative Details: The capital city and official languages provide practical knowledge for travellers, while the time zone is essential for planning itineraries.
- Symbols and Maps: The country’s flag and coat of arms reflect cultural identity, while the Google Maps link allows easy navigation planning.
- Demographic and Location Data: The population size and exact location help users understand the scale and positioning of each destination.

Users can save their favourite countries, adding them to a favourites list that focuses on crucial travel details: the capital city, continent, time zones (UTC), and Google Maps location. 
This feature allows travellers to quickly access important information about their preferred destinations.
The CRUD (Create-Read-Update-Delete) functionality in the World Explorer application enables users to manage personalized travel itineraries. Here’s how each CRUD component contributes to travel planning:
- Create: Users can create a new itinerary by for saving their destination choices details, travel dates, and planned activities.
- Read: Travelers can view their saved itineraries and country planning details to keep track of their plans.
- Update: Users can modify existing itineraries to adjust dates or activities as plans evolve.
- Delete: Unwanted or outdated itineraries can be removed, keeping the itinerary list organized and relevant.
Overall, World Explorer combines up-to-date country information with flexible trip planning tools, making it a powerful resource for travellers seeking to customize their travel experiences.
